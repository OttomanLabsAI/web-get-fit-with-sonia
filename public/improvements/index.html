Tale of the tape

getfitwithsonia.co.uk todayThe new site
Seven separate pages: Home, Classes Timetable, Online classes, Gallery, Testimonials, Contact and Book Now.One page with everything on it, in the order a new visitor needs it. The old page addresses still work and land on the right part of the page.
Twelve coloured class boxes in two columns, not in day order, with times written four different ways ("9.30 am-10.15 am", "12:15pm-13:00pm", "11:40am-12:40am").One timetable in day order with consistent times, the postcode and a map link for every session, today's classes marked, and a button that prints it on one sheet.
On a phone, the welcome text and the timetable boxes run off the right edge of the screen.Checked at every width from 320px to 1920px: nothing overflows, and the text reflows to fit.
Prices only on the Book Now page.£12 a session and £90 for ten shown at the top of the page and next to the classes.
Classes are named on the timetable but never described.A panel for each of the six classes: who it is for, what to expect, and when and where it runs.
The Facebook, X and Instagram icons link to nowhere.Real links, waiting on your confirmation of the right accounts.
The home page weighs about 2 MB: ten scripts, three stylesheets and fonts fetched from Google on every visit.The whole page is about 345 KB including seven photos: one stylesheet, one small script, fonts served from the site itself, nothing fetched from anyone else.
No page has a description for search engines, six of the seven have no main heading, and most photos have no text alternative.A title and description, one clear heading structure, every photo described, a sitemap, a sharing image for WhatsApp and Facebook, and structured data that tells Google your prices.
Runs on WordPress 6.2 from 2023 with the Divi theme: updates, plugins and a database to look after.Plain files on Cloudflare: nothing to update, nothing to patch, and hosting is free.
A contact form that posts to WordPress.Email and call buttons that open the visitor's own mail or phone app, and your number and email pinned to the bottom of every screen.
