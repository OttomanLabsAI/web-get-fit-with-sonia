Zumba

Who

Who it's for

Anyone who loves music and moving. Sonia is licensed to teach Zumba® and Zumba Gold®, so there is a pace for beginners, active older adults and seasoned dancers alike.

What

What to expect

Latin and world rhythms with easy-to-follow routines. No dance experience needed, just follow Sonia and enjoy it.

When

Your instructor

Hello and welcome

I'm Sonia Tonge and I've been a Fitness Instructor for almost 15 years now, and I absolutely love teaching a variety of classes, from keep fit, Zumba, legs bums and tums, stretch and hula hooping to chair exercise.

The reason is simple: I don't feel like it's a job, as I feel so good after each class and, even better still, I love to see my participants getting the same enjoyment. I feel like I have a bigger family, as some have become good friends and have been with me from the start. Spreading joy while exercising is the most amazing feeling.

If you feel you have lost your sparkle and want it back, come and join us in one of the classes. I look forward to seeing you in class.
