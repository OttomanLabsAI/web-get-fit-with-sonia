What Sonia teaches

Classes

Six kinds of class, one friendly instructor. Pick the one that suits you, or mix and match through the week. Every class is to music, and you work at your own pace.

Keep Fit

Who

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a friendly, all-round workout at a pace they can manage. Beginners are welcome, and you work at your own level.

What

What to expect

A warm-up, easy-to-follow moves to music to get your heart going, some toning work and a good stretch to finish.

When

When and where

Monday 12:15pm at Ruth Winston Centre, Palmers Green. Wednesday 10:00am at the United Reformed Church, Winchmore Hill. Friday 10:00am at Grange Park Methodist Church. Other sessions on the timetable may run keep fit too, so ask Sonia.

Ask about Keep Fit

Zumba & Zumba Gold

Who

Who it's for

Zumba is a dance-fitness party for anyone who loves music and moving. Zumba Gold is the gentler version, made for active older adults, beginners and anyone who wants the fun at a lower intensity.

What

What to expect

Merengue, salsa, cumbia, flamenco, tango, belly dance and world rhythms. No dance experience needed, just follow Sonia and enjoy it.

When

When and where

Sonia is a licensed Zumba® and Zumba Gold® instructor. Ask her which sessions on the timetable are running Zumba this term.

Your instructor

Meet Sonia

Sonia Tonge has been teaching fitness classes for almost 15 years. She is a qualified gym instructor with a dance background and a licensed Zumba® instructor, and has been a member of the Zumba Instructor Network since 2012.

She lives and works in Winchmore Hill and teaches ten classes a week close to home, in church halls, a community centre and a library across Winchmore Hill, Southgate, Palmers Green, Enfield and Barnet.

Sonia describes her classes as a bigger family, with plenty of regulars who have become good friends, and says that spreading joy while exercising is an amazing feeling. If you feel you have lost your sparkle and want it back, she would love you to come and join one of the classes.

“The feel good vibe will rub off on you and you will come back for more.”
