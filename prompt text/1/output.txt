The improved site is built, verified and pushed to `claude/website-improvement-lxa3wt`. It is not on `main` yet, so nothing has deployed. Fast-forward `main` when you are happy with it.

**What changed.** The old site spread the timetable, gallery, testimonials, contact and booking across separate pages. The new site puts everything a new face needs on one fast page, in the house style: a hero with the week at a glance and the two ways to reach Sonia, a facts strip, one tab per kind of class, the full Monday-to-Friday timetable with postcodes and map links, an about section with Sonia's credentials and her own words, a card per venue, and a contact band. The classes are keep fit, Zumba and Zumba Gold, legs bums and tums, stretch, hula hooping and chair exercise. The page prints as a one-sheet timetable, marks today's classes, self-hosts its fonts, sends nothing to third parties, ships a strict content-security policy, a sitemap, Open Graph tags and a sharing image, and works without JavaScript.

**How it was verified.** The wrangler dry run passes. The layout verifier sweeps both routes from 320px to 1920px with the real fonts loaded: no overflow, no wrapped single-word headings. axe reports zero accessibility violations on both pages at desktop and phone widths. html-validate is clean. I looked at full-page screenshots at 320px, 390px, 600px, 900px and 1440px, and at the printed PDF.

**What I could not verify.** The live site is blocked by this environment's egress policy, as were the Wayback Machine, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, zumba.com and the venue websites. Every fact was recovered from search-engine snippets of the site's own pages and a few directory listings, and each one is recorded with its source and confidence in `work/brief.json`. No brand colours, logo or photos could be captured, so the page runs on the house defaults and stays typographic until Sonia supplies photos.
